NetMap Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

NetMap is an offline-first network inventory and topology management application.

Information We Collect

NetMap does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share any personal information.

Data Storage

All data entered into NetMap is stored locally on your device using a private SQLite database.

This may include device names, IP addresses, ports, groups, topology information, notes, and templates.

Data Sharing

NetMap does not automatically share any data with the developer or third parties.

Exported files such as XML, CSV, PDF, PNG, or encrypted backups are created only when initiated by the user.

Internet Access

NetMap is designed to operate offline and does not require an internet connection for core functionality.

Purchases

NetMap may offer a one-time “NetMap Pro” in-app purchase.

Purchase processing is handled by Google Play. NetMap does not receive or store payment card information.

Children

NetMap is intended for professional and technical users and is not directed toward children.

Security

Users are responsible for securing their device and exported backup files.
